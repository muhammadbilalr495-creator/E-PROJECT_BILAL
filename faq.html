FAQ

Your questions, freshly answered.

01Do you offer eggless products?

Yes! Look for the Eggless label on our menu or ask our team about available options.

02Can I order a custom cake?

Absolutely. Contact us with your design, flavour and date. Please order at least 2 days in advance.

03Do you deliver?

We deliver across Karachi. Delivery is free on orders above Rs. 3,000. Contact us to confirm your area.

04How should I store my cake?

Keep cakes refrigerated and follow the shelf-life details listed for each product.

05Do your products contain allergens?

Some products contain gluten, dairy, eggs and nuts. Please check the ingredients and contact us about any allergies.

06How do I place an order?

Choose your treats from the menu, then contact us by phone or email to confirm your order.

07What are your opening hours?

We’re open Monday to Sunday, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

08Can I buy merchandise?

Yes, visit our Merchandise page for branded mugs, tote bags, glasses and serving trays.
